Course Notes

A selection of summarised lecture notes compiled during coursework in mathematics and computer science. These notes are intended more as revision aids and references and NOT comprehensive scribes. Emphasis has been laid on clarity and succinctness. I typically write these notes when preparing for the respective course examinations and doing so offers hindsight benefit, being the second iteration of reading the topic.

As an unintended consequence, length of a notes document indicates how much time has been spent preparing for that course. If I'm studying regularly the notes will be extensive and if I'm starting a night before, the notes will (unfortunately) reflect that. It was amusing though, to note the surprising lack of correlation between time spent studying for a course and the performance thereafter.
