Where's my NatGeo picture award?

Contrary to what the existence of this page may imply, I am NOT an avid photographer. Having said that, I do believe in the power of pictures to narrate stories and forge memories. All of us live uniquely beautiful lives and while not every moment of them can be shared, some can and deserve to be captured and reminisced upon.

Thus, given my affinity for travelling and admiration of air transit in particular, I often find my phone's gallery filled to the brim with intriguing photos. I cherish all of them equally but some in particular stand out, either by virtue of their quality or the substance.

So feel free to take a stroll down my memory lane, comprising of my travels in India and abroad - from posh cities to rural retreats. I've visited 11 countries thus far across 4 continents but I still adore Mumbai the most.
